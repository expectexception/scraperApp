  • Operations Controller

    Apply

    Hi Fly is a leading widebody aircraft wet lease specialist operating worldwide, with an Airbus fleet of A330 and A340.

    The wet lease business market is very demanding, and airlines are increasingly opting for wet lease solutions as they are found to be reliable, safe, and efficient. For that reason, Hi Fly fleet is steadily growing with new widebody additions in the coming months.

    Considering the forecasted growth, we are currently looking for highly motivated Operations Controllers to join our Integrated Operations Control Centre (iOCC) in Lisbon-Portugal.

    The Integrated Operations Control Center (iOCC) is at the heart of our airline daily activity. The iOCC coordinates day-to-day and minute-to-minute flight and ground operations. It provides centralized, real-time operational control and management of overall aircraft movement control, dispatch, maintenance control and crew tracking.

    Operations Controllers are in the driving seat for efficient and smooth operations and execution of the planned schedules. They track - in real time - all company activity from departure to arrival, manage irregularities and engage when complex and uncertain operational disruptive situations occur. The role must be viewed as very dynamic and demanding as it is critical to develop suitable solutions for any irregularity that arises, ensuring that when disruptions occur, corrective actions are taken to return the flight schedule to normal. Operations Control is not only reactive but also preventive and proactive in the way it works.

    The reporting line is to the Head of OCC.

     

    What will be your main responsibilities:

    • Monitoring and coordinating daily activity.
    • Flight following and in-flight assistance to flight crews.
    • Take informed decisions when unplanned operations occur, in a Collaborative Decision Making (CDM) environment, after consulting all relevant operational areas.
    • Close coordination with the Maintenance Department concerning unscheduled maintenance events and MEL restrictions that may affect aircraft operations.
    • Outside normal working hours, manage handling, overflight permits and services coordination related to flights.
    • Communicate with customers and assist the Commercial Department on any new ACMI or Charter requests, outside normal working hours.
    • Evaluate and initiate activation of the Company ERP.

     

    Some of our requirements:

    • Working experience in a similar role with other airlines (preferably).
    • Ideally have a background in the aviation industry, such as operations, flight dispatch, handling.
    • Capacity to work well in a fast-paced and demanding environment and be flexible to the changing needs of the business.
    • Successful candidates will maintain a high level of professionalism while multi-tasking various real time issues in a fast-paced environment.
    • Must have excellent analytical and creative thinking.
    • Have a high level of professionalism and the ability to interact with all levels of leadership.
    • Must be detail oriented and able to work productively, independently or within a team.
    • Multicultural awareness, excellent interpersonal skills, and a clear communication style.
    • Fast learner and highly motivated.
    • Good commercial awareness.
    • Fluent in spoken and written English is mandatory.
    • Good proficiency in using Microsoft Office suite and be able to learn new systems quickly.

     

    This is an office-based role in Lisbon-Portugal, working shift patterns, 24/7/365. Due to the nature of the wet lease operations (ACMI and Charter), this is not an ordinary office job, and you can expect very demanding, challenging, and exciting days upon joining our Organization.

  • Junior First Officer Program

    Apply

    HI FLY is a leading aircraft wet lease specialist operating worldwide, with Airbus widebody aircraft.

    The wet lease business market is very demanding, and airlines are increasingly opting for wet lease solutions as they are found to be reliable, safe, and efficient. For that reason, Hi Fly fleet is steadily growing with new widebody additions in the coming months.

    Are you ready to take flight and begin your journey as a professional pilot? Hi Fly is offering an unparalleled opportunity for a partially self-sponsored Junior First Officer Program for aspiring pilots to soar into their careers.


    Why Become a Hi Fly Pilot?

    • Comprehensive Type Rating Training: Gain world-class training, including computer-based, ground school and continuous simulator sessions led by experienced instructors. Get certified on a widebody aircraft with guidance from expert instructors and examiners to achieve your full potential.
    • Diverse Line Training: Fly across our extensive global network, gaining experience at a variety of airports worldwide.
    • Career Growth: Unfreeze your license with Hi Fly and advance through our “Route to Command” program.
    • Global Exposure: Experience transatlantic flying, ETOPS, and the excitement of piloting to unique destinations across the globe.
    • Attractive Work-Life Balance: Enjoy a 22 days on / 10 days off roster pattern, with company-paid positioning flights to and from your home base.
    • Early Career Compensation:

    - Earn a salary starting from Ground School completion.

    - Benefit from a step-change fixed base salary, variable pay based on block hours, and per diem when outside your home base.


    Minimum Requirements:

    To be eligible for the Hi Fly Junior First Officer Program, you must meet the following criteria:

    • Right to Work: Hold unrestricted right to live and work in the EU.
    • Language Proficiency: Minimum ICAO English Level 5
    • License:

    - Valid EASA CPL or Frozen ATPL.

    - Valid Multi-Engine Instrument Rating.

    • Medical: Hold a valid EU (Part-MED) issued Class 1 medical certificate.
    • Additional Certifications:

    - Certificate of satisfactory completion of Advanced Upset Prevention and Recovery Training (AUPRT) in accordance with FCL.745.A.

    - Certificate of satisfactory completion of Multi-Crew Co-Operation (MCC) in accordance with AMC1 FCL.735.A.

    - Candidates with Airline Pilot Standards Multi-Crew Cooperation (APS MCC) certification, in accordance with AMC2 FCL.735.A, will have a distinct advantage in the interview and assessment process.

    • Flight School (ATO) Report:

    - Written in English, signed by the Flight School Head of Training.

    - Must include all ATPL results (with details of retakes), CPL & ME/IR series passes, and information about your performance throughout training.

     

    Your future in aviation begins now. Are you ready to take the leap?
